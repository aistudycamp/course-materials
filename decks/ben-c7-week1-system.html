Before you build it

Two rules

Lowercase, no spaces

Hyphens are fine. A space in the name means quoting every path later.

Top of your user folder

Not Desktop, not Documents. Both sync automatically and that causes problems later.

~2 min Two small rules, and they are worth getting right now instead of fixing later. Lowercase, no spaces, hyphens if you want a break. And it goes at the top level of your own user folder, not Desktop or Documents. If you already made a folder for this course somewhere else this week, this is where it moves to, and that move is the lesson, not a chore.
